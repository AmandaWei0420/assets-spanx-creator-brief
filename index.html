How to film it

Hook, Show, Prove, Recommend

Make it feel like a real recommendation to a friend, not a scripted ad.

Hook

Show a featured style in the first 1–3 seconds. Lead with a need or benefit shoppers care about.

Show

Full-body view of each look. Natural movement, multiple angles and close-ups.

Prove

Share your real experience, not a feature list. One clear benefit and one real reason you'd wear it.

Recommend

End with a genuine endorsement and a direct CTA to shop the styles on TikTok Shop.

Language to use: Simple solutions · Quality basics · Everyday comfort · Accessible value
